Phase 01–02 · Shipped

The first two phases gave every project a real home.

Before this, a project's details lived wherever I'd last left them — a registrar tab, a note to myself, a half-remembered .env file. Phases one and two fixed that: a place to create a project, and a shape rich enough to hold everything that belongs to it.

Phase 03 · Active now

Turning stored data into something worth checking.

This phase is the gap between having the data and understanding it — what needs attention, what's missing, where everything actually lives. It's roughly 80% done; what's left is bug-fixing and polish on top of what's already shipped.
